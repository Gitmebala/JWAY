JWAY

Walking directions across JKUAT's Juja campus — down to the room, spoken aloud so you can keep your head up and your phone in your pocket.

Using it while you walk

  • Tap ◎ to start from where you're standing.
  • Say it instead of typing — the mic takes "take me to ELB 212".
  • Hit Start and pocket the phone. It announces every turn, buzzes, and holds the screen awake until you arrive.
  • Room codes carry their own floor: CTC 207 is the 2nd floor, CTC 105 the 1st, CTC 03 the ground.

Where the data comes from

Buildings, footpaths, street names and points of interest come from OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed ODbL, filled out with a public JKUAT ArcGIS survey — the two agreed to within 4–16 m, which is how they were checked against each other. Room codes were harvested from JKUAT's own published exam timetables. Satellite imagery is Esri World Imagery.

CTC's block is unnamed in OpenStreetMap, so its location came from someone who walks past it. Adding it upstream would make that permanent for every map, not just this one.
